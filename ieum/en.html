Ieum: a free planner that puts your to-dos on a 24-hour clock

iPhone · Free · Coming soon to the App Store

Ieum (이음) is a free planner app for iPhone that combines a to-do list with a 24-hour circular timetable. Your day is drawn as one turn of a clock, and your to-dos sit inside its time blocks (time blocking). Repeating tasks, multiple alarms, stats with deadlines, a Pomodoro focus timer with background sounds, themes and a Home Screen widget are all free, with no subscription or in-app purchase; the app is supported by ads. There is no account, and your data stays on your device.

Features

24-hour circular timetable

  • The whole day on one clock face: midnight at the top, noon at the bottom
  • A hand shows the current time and the current block
  • A different timetable for each weekday; copy a day to other days
  • Drag block handles in 5-minute steps; overlaps are flagged before saving
  • Switch to a vertical timeline if you prefer a list

To-dos and repeats

  • Daily, weekly (chosen weekdays), monthly (a date or "second Tuesday"), every N days
  • Duration, icon and a target deadline
  • No limit on the number of tasks
  • Missed one-time tasks get a suggested free slot today

Alarms

  • Any mix of 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 minutes before and on time

Stats

  • For each goal: progress so far and progress against the deadline
  • Category donut, weekday bars, trend chart and a one-year heatmap
  • Week, month and year views

Calendar and stickers

  • Monthly calendar with each day's progress
  • Five sticker packs: Basic, Mood, Study, Season, Workout
  • Show device calendar events (optional, read only)

Focus

  • Pomodoro or countdown timer
  • Background sounds: rain, café, forest, white noise (keeps playing with the screen off)
  • No ads during a focus session

Make it yours

  • Seven theme colours (Peach, Ocean, Forest, Lavender, Cherry, Lemon, Mono), light and dark
  • Three app icons and a Home Screen widget with today's tasks

Help and backup

  • A searchable how-to guide in Settings
  • One backup file moves everything to a new iPhone

FAQ

What is Ieum?

An iPhone planner that combines a to-do list with a 24-hour circular timetable. You split the day into time blocks and put your to-dos inside them. Checking a task on the clock checks it in the list too.

Is it really free? Is there a subscription?

Every feature is free. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no paid "remove ads" option, and no feature is unlocked by watching ads. The app is supported by Google AdMob ads.

Where do ads appear?

A banner at the bottom of some screens and occasional full-screen ads (when the app opens, when you enter Stats, after a focus session ends). There are no ads while you add a task, edit the timetable or focus, or right after you check a task.

Can I use it for time blocking?

Yes. Split each weekday into blocks (for example sleep, deep work, exercise) and put tasks in each block. Drag the handles to change times in 5-minute steps and copy a day to other weekdays.

Where are my tasks and timetable stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, so the developer cannot see your data. Device calendar events are read only when you turn that on, and are never changed or sent anywhere.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Use Profile and settings (Appearance) › Backup › Create backup file to save one file to Files, iCloud Drive or a message, then Restore from backup on the new iPhone.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Ieum is on iPhone now, and an Android version is in preparation.

Which languages does it support?

English and Korean. It follows your iPhone's language.

Privacy

There is no account, and your tasks, timetable and stats stay on your device. Google AdMob may process device advertising identifiers to show ads; every feature works the same whether or not you allow tracking. Full privacy policy
