Privacy Policy — Ganeum

Effective: 2026-11-22 · NJHouse

Ganeum (“the app”) estimates which business types and franchise brands may suit a specific store unit in Korea, using public data and franchise disclosure documents for the building you pick on the map.

There are no accounts, no ads, and we run no servers. The developer does not receive your location, your searches, the stores you save or the conditions you enter.

1. What we do not collect

The developer collects no user data. None of the following is ever sent to the developer:

  • your current location or the buildings you look at on the map
  • your search words
  • the store conditions you enter (floor area, deposit, rent, key money, investment)
  • the stores you save, their results, and your field-check notes
  • names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, or any identity information

Scores and profit estimates are calculated on your device with fixed formulas. The app does not use AI to analyze you or your data.

2. Data sent to outside services to make the app work

To show maps and public data, the app sends requests directly from your device to the services below. These requests contain the search words you type, or the map coordinates, address and area codes of the building you pick — not your name, any account, or the store conditions you enter. The developer does not receive these requests.

3. Location

  • The app uses your location only when you tap the “Current location” button and allow location permission.
  • The coordinates are used once to move the map and to find nearby buildings (sent to Kakao, VWorld and the public data APIs above). They are not stored and are not sent to the developer.
  • If you deny location permission, you can still pick a building on the map or search for an address.

The app shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK, no advertising ID use, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and no feature is locked. Brand rankings are never influenced by advertising or partnerships.
