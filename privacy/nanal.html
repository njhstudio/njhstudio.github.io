The selected photo is copied into the app’s private folder, so deleting the original from your gallery does not remove it from your diary. Conversely, deleting a diary entry removes that copy.

The app is free and is funded by Google AdMob.

In serving ads, Google and its partners may collect your advertising ID, device information, approximate location (from IP address), and app usage information. This is collected by Google, not by us; the developer has no access to it.

  • Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.

EEA / UK users: consent is requested via Google’s UMP before any personalized ad request. You can change your choice at any time in the app’s settings.

Your diary content is never used for advertising and is never passed to the ad provider.

No ads appear while you write or read an entry. A single banner sits at the bottom of the calendar screen; the writing and detail screens contain no ad at all.

6. Google Drive backup (optional)

This is the one feature that sends your diary off the device. It is off until you turn it on, and it never runs by itself.

  • Upload happens only when you tap it. There is no automatic or background sync.
  • The backup file goes to your own Google Drive, not to us. The developer has no server and no access to it.
  • The app requests only the drive.file scope. This grants access to files this app itself created — it cannot read, change or even see any other file in your Drive.
  • Uploaded backups are visible in your Drive and you can delete them yourself at any time.
  • The app keeps the 5 most recent backups and removes older ones so your Drive does not fill up.
  • Disconnecting stops any further upload. Files already uploaded remain in your Drive; delete them yourself if you no longer want them.

While connected, Google provides the app with your account’s email address. It is used only to show you which account is connected and is transmitted nowhere.

A backup file contains your photos and writing as they are, and is not encrypted. Please do not share it with others.

8. Sharing with third parties

We collect no personal information, so there is nothing for us to share. Ad-related processing follows Google’s policy referenced in section 5. Drive backup goes to your own account as described in section 6.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no information identifying you, there is no separate access or deletion request process.

  • Entries can be edited or deleted in the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling removes everything stored on the device.
