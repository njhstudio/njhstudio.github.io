3. Data stored on your device

Project data (clip order, subtitle text, effect settings), paths to the media you selected, narration recordings, copies of fonts you added, and automatically generated low-resolution preview copies. None of this is transmitted. All of it is removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app is free and is funded by Google AdMob. In serving ads, Google and its partners may collect your advertising ID, device information, approximate location (from IP address), and app usage information. This is collected by Google, not by us; the developer has no access to it.

  • Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.

EEA / UK users: consent is requested via Google’s UMP before any personalized ad request. You can change your choice at any time in the app’s Settings → Reset ad consent.

No ads are shown on the editing screen. This is a deliberate decision to avoid accidental taps while you work.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

6. Sharing with third parties

We collect no personal information, so there is nothing for us to share. Ad-related processing follows Google’s policy referenced in section 4.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no information identifying you, there is no separate access or deletion request process. You can remove everything stored on your device by deleting projects in the app or uninstalling it.

유럽경제지역(EEA)·영국 이용자
