Privacy Policy — Dure

Effective: 2026-10-10 · NJHouse

Dure (“the app”) splits bills and settles up with the people you shared them with.

There are no accounts, and we run no servers.

1. What we do not collect

The developer collects no user data. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, and no user identifier. Specifically, none of the following ever reaches the developer:

  • group names, member names, expense titles, memos
  • amounts, split results, who owes whom
  • receipt photos
  • bank account numbers
  • names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, or any identity information

2. What is stored on your device

WhatWhere
Groups, members, expenses, splits, settlement historyApp database on device
Receipt photos you attachApp storage on device
Bank account numbers you enterApp database on device — never included in a shared code
Settings (currency, rounding unit, your name)App preferences on device

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Backup files you create yourself remain where you saved them.

3. Network use

The app uses the internet in exactly two situations:

PurposeWho it goes toWhat is sent
Exchange ratesapi.frankfurter.app, api.exchangerate.hostOne base-currency code (for example KRW). Nothing else.
AdsGoogle AdMobSee section 4

Those two rate services necessarily see your IP address while handling the request — that is a property of all internet traffic, not something the app sends. Group names, member names, amounts and device identifiers are not part of the request.

Requests to any address outside that allow-list are blocked inside the app. Everything else — splitting, settling, sharing, statistics, backup — works offline.

4. Advertising

The app is free and is paid for by Google AdMob ads. There are no paid features, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases.

To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • the advertising identifier (Android advertising ID)
  • IP address, and the approximate location inferred from it
  • device information (model, OS version, language)
  • ad impressions, clicks and other interactions
  • diagnostic information generated while the app is used

This is collected by Google. What reaches the developer is aggregated revenue statistics only, from which individual users cannot be identified.

  • Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: device Settings → Google → Ads

If you are in the EEA or the UK, a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form is shown before the first ad request. Declining does not restrict any feature of the app; ads are then shown in a non-personalised form. You can change that choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy settings.

5. Permissions

6. About the payment-app buttons

The settlement screen has buttons that open Toss, KakaoPay, Venmo, PayPal, Wise or Revolut, with apps you already have listed first.

The app does not read the list of programs installed on your device. Android offers a permission that enumerates every installed app (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES); this app does not use it. What the app does is ask the system, for those six apps only, “can this be opened?” — and those six are written out in AndroidManifest.

The answer is used only to order the buttons on screen, and never leaves the device.

7. Sharing with other people (Dure Code)

You can share a group as a QR code, a link, or a file. That data goes directly to the other device without passing through any server, and the developer cannot see it.

A shared code never contains bank account numbers. Those stay on your device.

Whatever you use to deliver the code (messenger, e-mail and so on) is governed by that service’s own privacy policy. Anyone who receives the QR code or link can read the expenses inside it, so take care whom you share it with.

8. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until you delete it. Deleting a group or an expense inside the app, or uninstalling the app, deletes it. The developer keeps no copy, so data deleted from the device cannot be recovered.

9. Children

This app is not directed primarily at children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. Changes that widen what is collected are announced separately inside the app.
