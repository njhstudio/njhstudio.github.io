Effective: 2026-09-13

1. Summary

  • No account with us, no sign-up. We create no identifier for you.
  • We operate no server. The videos you save never reach us, and we cannot see them.
  • Saved videos stay on your device, in your own gallery.
  • Instagram login is optional and only needed for stories and highlights. You type your credentials into Instagram’s own page; this app never sees or stores them. See section 4.
  • The app displays ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect certain information to serve them. See section 5.

4. Instagram login (optional)

Reels and public posts save without logging in. Stories and highlights need a login because Instagram only shows them to logged-in viewers — there is no way around this that does not involve a session.

  • Logging in is entirely optional. The app never asks for it on startup, and no feature other than stories depends on it.
  • You log in on Instagram’s own page, shown inside the app. We do not build the username and password fields, and this app cannot see, read or store what you type there.
  • What remains afterwards is a session cookie held by your device. We have no server, so it goes nowhere.
  • Logging out in Settings → Instagram login deletes that cookie.
  • While you are not logged in, the app does not request stories at all — it tells you a login is needed instead of sending a request that would fail.

Using a logged-in session to fetch content may conflict with Instagram’s own terms of service. If that matters to you, leave this feature alone and keep saving reels and public posts.

The app is free and supported by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may process:

This processing is performed by Google under the Google Advertising Policies.

Personalised ads can be turned off. In the EEA and the UK a consent form appears on first launch. Elsewhere you can reset or opt out of your advertising ID in system settings. No feature is withheld if you opt out.

6. Copyright of saved content

Rights in the videos you save belong to their creators. This app is a tool for personal viewing and archiving. Obtain permission before reposting or using content commercially; that responsibility is yours.

7. Children

This app is rated for ages 13 and over on Google Play and is not directed to younger children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Korean law (the Personal Information Protection Act) sets its own threshold at 14, requiring a legal guardian’s consent below that age. Because this app collects no personal information at all, no such consent is required either way. Where the two ages differ we apply the stricter one.

8. Sharing with third parties

The app collects no personal information, so there is nothing for us to share. Advertising is handled by Google under section 5. The optional Instagram session stays on your device as described in section 4.

9. Your rights

We hold no information that identifies you, so there is no separate access or deletion request procedure.

  • Saved items can be deleted in the app or in your gallery at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the device.
  • The Instagram session can be cleared at any time in Settings → Instagram login → Log out.
  • The advertising ID can be reset in system Settings → Privacy → Ads.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we will say so through an app update and this page. Changes that add anything to what is collected will be announced before they take effect.

  • 로그인은 전적으로 선택입니다. 앱 실행 시 로그인을 요구하지 않으며, 스토리 외의 기능은 로그인 여부와 무관합니다.
  • 로그인은 앱 안에 표시되는 인스타그램 자체 화면에서 이루어집니다. 아이디와 비밀번호 입력란을 본 앱이 만들지 않으며, 본 앱은 입력 내용을 보거나 읽거나 저장할 수 없습니다.
  • 이후 남는 것은 기기가 보관하는 세션 쿠키뿐입니다. 저희는 서버가 없으므로 어디로도 전송되지 않습니다.

로그인 세션으로 콘텐츠를 가져오는 것은 인스타그램의 이용약관에 어긋날 수 있습니다. 이 점이 우려되시면 이 기능을 사용하지 않고 릴스와 공개 게시물만 저장하셔도 됩니다.
