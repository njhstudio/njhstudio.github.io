Privacy Policy — Hearim

Effective: 2026-08-27

1. Summary

  • No account, no sign-in. We do not create any identifier for you.
  • We operate no server. The app sends none of your data to us.
  • Your transactions, budgets and balances stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • We do not read your SMS. The app never requests SMS permission. It only reads a message you paste or share to it yourself.
  • The app displays ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect certain information to serve them. See section 5.

2. Permissions

That is the complete list of what we ask for. The app requests no access to SMS, call logs, contacts, photos, location or the microphone.

Three further permissions are declared by the libraries we use, and Play lists them alongside ours: vibration (haptic feedback on the number pad and for notifications), wake lock and foreground service (used by the notification and ad libraries). None of them send data anywhere.

3. Data stored on your device

Transactions, accounts and cards, budgets, recurring rules, categories, and app settings — kept in a database inside the app’s private storage. If you turn on the app lock, your PIN is stored only as a salted hash; the number itself is never saved. None of this is transmitted. All of it is removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Reading payment messages

The app can turn a card-payment message into a transaction, but only from text you hand it — pasted from the clipboard, or shared to the app from your messaging app. It does not have, and does not ask for, permission to read your messages.

The text is parsed on the device. Neither the message nor anything read from it is sent anywhere. Nothing is saved until you confirm it.

5. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app is free and is funded by Google AdMob. In serving ads, Google and its partners may collect your advertising ID, device information, approximate location (from IP address), and app usage information. This is collected by Google, not by us; the developer has no access to it.

  • Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.
  • Your transactions are never sent to the ad SDK. Ads are not targeted using what you record.

Where ads never appear: the home screen and the entry sheet. No ad is shown right after you save. You can also watch one short video to remove ads for three hours.

6. Backups

A backup is a file you create, and you choose where it goes through the Android file picker. If you pick a cloud app (Google Drive, Dropbox and so on), the file goes to your storage under your own account — it does not pass through us. The app has no automatic or background backup.

7. Analytics and crash reporting

None. The app contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not know how many screens you opened or which features you use.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Sharing with third parties

We collect no personal information, so there is nothing for us to share. Ad-related processing follows Google’s policy referenced in section 5.

10. Your rights

Because we hold no information identifying you, there is no separate access or deletion request process. You can remove everything by deleting records in the app or uninstalling it. There is nowhere else to ask, because we hold nothing.

11. Changes

Changes will be announced through app updates and this page. Any change that adds a collected data category will be announced before it takes effect.

문자 해석은 기기 안에서 이루어집니다. 문자 내용도, 거기서 읽어낸 정보도 외부로 나가지 않습니다. 이용자가 확인하기 전에는 저장되지 않습니다.

5. 광고 — Google AdMob

본 앱은 무료로 제공되며, 운영 비용은 Google AdMob 광고로 충당합니다.

광고 제공 과정에서 Google 및 그 파트너는 광고 ID, 기기 정보, 대략적인 위치(IP 주소 기반), 앱 사용 정보 등을 수집·이용할 수 있습니다. 이는 본 앱이 아니라 Google이 수집하는 것이며, 본 앱 개발자는 해당 정보에 접근하지 않습니다.

  • Google의 정책: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • 광고 ID 재설정·삭제: 기기의 설정 → 개인정보 보호 → 광고 에서 언제든지 가능합니다.
  • 이용자의 거래 내역은 광고 SDK로 전달되지 않습니다. 기록한 내용을 근거로 광고를 맞추지 않습니다.

광고가 표시되지 않는 곳

홈 화면과 입력 화면에는 광고를 표시하지 않습니다. 매일 쓰는 자리이기 때문입니다. 저장한 직후에도 광고를 띄우지 않습니다.

짧은 영상을 하나 끝까지 보면 3시간 동안 광고가 나오지 않습니다.
